## Limits

New-letting medians can change with dwelling composition. These are nominal prices, not inflation-adjusted values. The source excludes certain accommodation types. Do not aggregate the LGA medians into a purported metropolitan median. No household income, transport, commute, boundaries or environment are included in this analysis. No forecasts, individual-property recommendations, vacancy estimates or causal effects have been established.
